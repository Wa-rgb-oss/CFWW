Services

CleanFreaks Window Washers LP provides our clients with a wide range of available options to maintain your home and vehicles. Here is a brief description of some of our services:

Window Services

Window services typically involve cleaning and maintaining windows to ensure they are clear, streak-free, and visually appealing. This may include both interior and exterior window cleaning using specialized equipment such as squeegees, cleaning solutions, and extension poles. Window services can also encompass the removal of dirt, debris, and hard water stains from glass surfaces, frames, and tracks.

Vehicle Services

Vehicle cleaning services encompass deep cleaning and restoration of both the interior and exterior surfaces of a vehicle. This includes cleaning and polishing the exterior paint, as well as treating tires and wheels. Inside, upholstery, carpets, and dashboard are cleaned and conditioned. Detailing aims to enhance your vehicle's appearance and protect its surfaces for long-term durability.

Pressure Washing Services

Pressure washing services focus on revitalizing outdoor surfaces and maintaining the cleanliness of your property. This involves driveways, sidewalks, decks, and siding using high-pressure water to remove dirt, grime, and mildew. Pressure washing helps preserve the longevity of surfaces by eliminating buildup that can cause damage over time. The goal is to keep your outdoor areas looking fresh and well-maintained, while protecting your property from unnecessary wear.
