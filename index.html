About CleanFreaks

Built around the details.

CleanFreaks started with window washing and grew into a broader cleaning service for homes, businesses, and vehicles. The common thread is attention to the parts people notice first: clean glass, clean surfaces, and a finished result.

Our services are designed to stay flexible. Whether it is a one-time clean, recurring window maintenance, vehicle care, or a larger property, the work is built around the job rather than forcing every customer into the same package.
