Cleaning FAQ

Quick answers about window cleaning, commercial service, recurring maintenance, quotes, and scheduling.

Does CleanFreaks clean both interior and exterior windows?

Yes. We can quote interior window cleaning, exterior window cleaning, or both depending on the property and requested scope.

Can screens and window tracks be included?

Yes. Screens, accessible tracks, glass doors, and related window details can be included when they are part of the written proposal.

Do you provide commercial window cleaning?

Yes. CleanFreaks provides commercial window cleaning and recurring glass maintenance for businesses and commercial properties in the Marietta area.

Do you offer recurring window cleaning?

Yes. Maintenance plans are available for clients who want scheduled recurring window care instead of starting from scratch each time.

When are cleaning appointments available?

Regular cleaning appointments are currently scheduled on Saturday and Sunday. You can submit a quote request online at any time.

How do I get a quote?

Use the free quote form to send your contact information, service address, requested service, and job details. We review the request and follow up with questions or a written proposal.
