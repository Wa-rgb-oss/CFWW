Why Choose A Subscription?

Our window cleaning subscription plan offers the perfect blend of convenience, value, and consistency. For just $34.99 a month, you'll enjoy quarterly cleanings that ensure your windows remain spotless and well-maintained throughout the year. No need to worry about scheduling last-minute cleanings, our structured quarterly visits allow us to service hundreds of clients efficiently, without sacrificing quality or attention to detail.

By subscribing, you'll benefit from priority service, meaning your home or business is guaranteed a spot on our carefully planned calendar. This consistency ensures that your windows are always cleaned on time, without the hassle of remembering to book each season. In addition, the predictable monthly cost makes budgeting easier, spreading out the cost of premium cleaning services evenly across the year.

Choosing a subscription also helps you lock in our services, avoiding seasonal rushes and price fluctuations. Our goal is to keep your windows sparkling clean, whether it's during busy summer months or after the stormy season. With our subscription, you'll experience peace of mind knowing your windows are professionally cared for year-round.

Trust CleanFreaks to provide a worry-free, affordable window cleaning solution that fits seamlessly into your schedule and budget!
